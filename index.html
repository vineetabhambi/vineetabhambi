Red Label Tea
FMCG · Emotional Brand · Spec Ad

The brief I set myself: make tea the hero of conflict resolution. Based on a real cultural observation — in Indian households, tea genuinely cools people down after arguments.

Built the entire emotional arc through sound design — raw street noise during the fight, soft sounds of boiling, pouring, grating ginger as tea brews, quiet ambience as conflict resolves. No dramatic music. Sound does the storytelling.

"Swad Apnapan Ka" isn't just a tagline — it's a cultural truth. Tea in India is how people say sorry without saying sorry.
Titan Nebula
Luxury Watches · Brand Equity · Spec Ad

Built around the double entendre of "showing time" — both as a literal function of a timepiece and a metaphorical indicator of personal status and legacy.

Macro shots, zero transitions, hard clean cuts. The only sound is the tick of the watch. Silence and stillness are the luxury signal.

Luxury is communicated through what you leave out, not what you put in.
